## Welcome to my website!


I'm a 4th year undergraduate student at USC (University of Southern California) studying mathematics.

![Me](image.JPG)


My current interest lies in algebraic topology, plus its connections to algebraic geometry, *K*-theory, number theory, and geometric topology. In particular, I'm working on a thesis about motivic cohomology and [*the Bloch–Kato conjecture*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norm_residue_isomorphism_theorem), so most mathematics I recently learned centers around it. 

My thesis advisor is [Aravind Asok](https://dornsife.usc.edu/aravind-asok/). I was also very fortunate to be mentored by [Joseph Helfer](https://www.jojhelfer.com/) and [Kyler Siegel](https://kylersiegel.xyz/) during my undergraduate study.

I have a side interest in theoretical computer science and combinatorics. I have been doing research centered around computational social choice for a while, with [Yusuf Kalayci](https://yhkalayci.github.io/), [David Kempe](https://www.david-kempe.com/), and [Evi Micha](https://evi-micha.github.io/).

This summer, I'm at the [UChicago Math REU](https://math.uchicago.edu/~may/REU2025/), where I'm mentored by [Peter May](https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~may/) and [Ajay Srinivasan](https://avsrinivasan.github.io/). I spent my past summers at [PCMI](https://www.ias.edu/pcmi/pcmi-2024-undergraduate-summer-school), [CTNT](https://ctnt-summer.math.uconn.edu/about-ctnt2024/), and [PROMYS](https://promys.org/).
